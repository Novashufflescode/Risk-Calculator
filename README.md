# Risk Calculator

A futures position size calculator with a Tokyo night city pop look (Nokyo Night palette).

Pick an instrument, your account size, your max risk and your stop. It tells you how many contracts to trade, your real dollar risk, your take profit, and (if you add your entry) the exact stop and target prices.

## Features

- **Instruments:** MNQ, MES, MYM, M2K, MGC, MCL (micro) and NQ, ES, YM, RTY, GC, CL (mini / full size)
- **Account presets** with suggested risk ranges (25K: $100 to $300, 50K: $250 to $500, larger accounts at about 0.5% to 1%)
- **Stop loss** from a dropdown or custom, in points or ticks (auto rounded to the nearest tick)
- **Take profit** by R multiple (1:1 up to 1:5) or custom points
- **Max contracts cap** for prop firm limits
- **Fees per contract** so risk and profit show net numbers
- **Long / Short + entry price** gives exact stop and target prices
- **Micro vs mini check:** shows what the same risk looks like on the other size
- **Reference table** like the GEM charts, highlighting what fits your risk and your exact setup
- **Copy trade plan** button for Discord or your journal
- **EN / ES** language toggle
- Saves your last setup on your device

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or use the live GitHub Pages link in the repo sidebar.
